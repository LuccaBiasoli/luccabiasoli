## 🧭 What I do

I work at the intersection of **product, business, data and technology**, leading digital products from discovery to delivery.

**Product.** I own the full lifecycle: strategy, discovery, roadmap, backlog, GTM and experimentation, acting as the bridge between business goals and technical execution across engineering, design, commercial and operations teams.

**E-commerce & Engineering.** Platform migrations and replatforming across **Adobe Commerce / Magento, Shopify, VTEX and AEM**, headless storefronts, checkout, catalog, integrations and performance work focused on **Core Web Vitals and conversion**, in LATAM and international operations.

**AI & Automation.** I treat AI as a **product capability**, not just a productivity tool: Generative AI grounded in real store data via APIs, agentic workflows, RAG, MCP-based integrations and AI-powered customer service.

> Understand the problem, validate the opportunity, define the right product, and stay close enough to the technology to actually build it.

**Business → Product → UX → Data → Architecture → APIs → Engineering → Delivery**
